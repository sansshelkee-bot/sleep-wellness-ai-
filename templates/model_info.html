Preprocessing Pipeline
  • Target Encoding: 3 balanced target classes: None (5,000), Insomnia (5,000), Sleep Apnea (5,000).
  • Blood Pressure Decomposition: Decomposes Blood Pressure (e.g. "120/80") into independent numerical features Systolic_BP and Diastolic_BP for continuous gradient modeling.
  • Numerical Scaling: StandardScaler applied across Age, Sleep Duration, Quality of Sleep, Physical Activity, Stress Level, Heart Rate, Daily Steps.
  • Categorical Encoding: OneHotEncoder(handle_unknown='ignore') for Gender, Occupation, and BMI Category.
  • SHAP TreeExplainer: Native multi-class Game-Theoretic TreeExplainer directly applied on CatBoost leaf weights for exact Shapley additive local feature attribution.
End-to-End System Pipeline
Step 1 User Ingestion: User enters 11 clinical & lifestyle attributes via Assessment UI.
Step 2 Preprocessing: Data transformed via saved preprocessor.pkl.
Step 3 Inference & Probabilities: CatBoost & Ensemble output calibrated confidence.
Step 4 Explainable AI: SHAP calculates real local feature contributions.
Step 5 Deterministic Scoring: Sleep Health Score (0-100) and Future Risk.
Step 6 Digital Twin Sync: State matrices updated; progress tracked over time.
